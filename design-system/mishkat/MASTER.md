# Mishkat: Design System (Master)

The source of truth for all store and dashboard pages. The tokens are implemented in `src/app/globals.css` (`@theme`).

## Identity
- **Mishkat (the brand):** ink black and gold, taken from the real logo (gold foil on black).
- **Sabili (the product):** royal blue and card yellow. These appear only inside the Sabili world (the Sabili section on the homepage, its product page, and the "how to play" section), so blue never becomes Mishkat's colour.
- **Signature element:** the *niche*, the pointed window from the logo and the Sabili box, used as a frame for product images (`.niche` / `.niche-sm`).
- **Card images:** the digital print files (`src/lib/card-images.ts`, generated by `npm run assets`) with corners rounded like the printed cards. The yellow and win cards are shown in landscape, like the real cards.
- **The one motion moment:** the fan of the five Sabili cards on the homepage (`CardFan`). It opens once, and only reduced motion disables it. Everything else stays still apart from hover and press feedback.

## Colors

## Type
- **Headings:** Aref Ruqaa (Ruqʿah Arabic calligraphy). It contrasts with the geometric Kufic of the Mishkat logo and the Sabili wordmark. The display sizes need a line-height of at least 1.35, set in `@theme`.
- **Body text:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic, 16px, line-height 1.75.
- Prices and numbers use tabular figures (`.tabular`).

## Layout
- RTL everywhere (`<html dir="rtl" lang="ar-TN">`), with logical properties only (`ms/me/ps/pe/start/end`).
- Container: `max-width: 76rem`, gutter 16px on mobile and 32px from 768px.
- Breakpoints: 640 / 768 / 1024 / 1280.
- Touch targets ≥ 44px, and a visible focus ring (gold-deep).

## Deliberately avoided
- Gradients for decoration, emoji as icons, a fade-in animation on every section, identical cards for every piece of content.
- Numbering, except for genuinely sequential content (the game stages).
- Made-up information: anything not confirmed is left out or left empty for the admin to fill in.
